What is checked?

Every check is listed in the report as Pass, Fail, Partial or Not verifiable, together with the observed and expected value. Severity: 1 Critical · 2 High/Medium · 3 Low.

How it works

Network + dataLayer parsing

GA4 /g/collect, Google Ads /pagead/…, Meta /tr, TikTok /api/v2/pixel, LinkedIn and X requests are decoded hit by hit; every hit is inspected separately.

Compared with official schemas

Required/recommended parameters, data types and formats from each platform's official documentation are checked.

Consent scenarios

Requests, cookies and gcs/gcd signals before consent, after accepting and after rejecting are tested separately.

Honest limits

Server-side setups (CAPI, Events API) are not visible from the browser; these checks are never guessed and are marked "Not verifiable".
